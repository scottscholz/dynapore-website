How it works

From vesicles to data in four steps.

{{svg:step-form}}01

Form

Add 5 µL of your vesicles. The bilayer forms on the chip.

{{svg:step-add}}02

Add

Add your LNPs, peptides or proteins.

{{svg:step-read}}03

Read

Impedance tracks the membrane in real time.

{{svg:step-look}}04

Look

Optional: image the same membrane on a slide.
