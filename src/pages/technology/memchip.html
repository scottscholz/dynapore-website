Step 1 · Sample prep

Vesicles with standard lab equipment.

Nothing specific to MemChip: glass vials, a nitrogen stream, a vortex mixer and a mini-extruder.

  1. Dispense lipid stocks into a glass vial
  2. Dry the lipid film under nitrogen, then vacuum
  3. Hydrate in buffer and vortex
  4. Extrude 11 passes through a 100 nm membrane
  5. Collect: about 1–6 mM lipid, ready for the chip
Step 2 · On the chip

A stable membrane in about 30 minutes.

01

Prepare the electrode

Ethanol soak, rinse with buffer. Optional baseline EIS.

02

Add 5 µL vesicles

Covering the working electrode. Wait 2 minutes.

03

Seal and wash

Mix in 5 µL sealing solution, wait 5 minutes, then wash gently.

04

Insert and record

Into the SPE adapter: membrane EIS, then a time course at 270 Hz.

~10 min · pre-treatment + baseline~20 min · stable bilayer formation
Step 3 · The readout

Impedance spectroscopy, on equipment labs already have.

  • Confirm the membrane with an EIS spectrum
  • Follow it continuously at 270 Hz, or with your own protocol
  • Runs on EIS-capable potentiostats via the SPE adapter

Which lipids can I use?

How long until first data?

About 30 minutes from a dry chip to a stable membrane: roughly 10 minutes of pre-treatment and baseline, 20 minutes of bilayer formation.
