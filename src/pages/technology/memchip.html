The principle

A real membrane, read electrically.

Stable

Tethers anchor the bilayer to our membrane surface, so it holds for days instead of minutes.

Your lipids

The bilayer forms from vesicles of your chosen composition and stays fluid, like a cell membrane.

Label-free, real time

Impedance tracks membrane integrity continuously. No dyes, no fluorescence, no washing steps.

Workflow

From vesicles to data in under half an hour.

Vesicles are made with standard lab equipment. Trial samples come with a step-by-step guide. On the chip, they fuse into a sealed bilayer within minutes, ready to measure.

The readout

Impedance spectroscopy on a standard potentiostat.

EIS-capable potentiostats are common equipment: many institutes already have one somewhere, often in an electrochemistry or biophysics group nearby.

FAQ

Before you request.

Which lipids can I use?

Your choice of composition, as extruded vesicles at about 1–6 mM lipid (about 0.8–5 mg/mL). {{tbc:any limits?}}

How long until first data?

Under half an hour from a dry chip to a stable membrane: pre-treatment and baseline, then about 5–10 minutes of bilayer formation per chip.
